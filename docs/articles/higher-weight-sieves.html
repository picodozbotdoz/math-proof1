Classical congruences, new formal proofs

Five more Ramanujan congruences, and a sieve that replaces computation

Weights 16 to 26 get their own τ-style theory. Their large moduli let a single residue test prove nonvanishing up to 1.7 million with no dense computation at all.

WHAT IS PROVED

For each of the five eigenforms below: integer coefficients, Hecke eigenform equation, Mordell multiplicativity, Lehmer's reduction to primes, a congruence with a divisor sum, and nonvanishing of every coefficient below a stated bound. All of it is in light modules, with no dense computation.

The space of cusp forms Sk(SL₂(ℤ)) is one-dimensional exactly for k = 12, 16, 18, 20, 22, 26. Each has a unique normalized form, and Δ at weight 12 is only the most famous. Lehmer's question makes sense for all six and is open for all six. Once the theory was written for a generic rank-one space (EigenLevelOne), each weight needed only its own congruence.

The sieve runs without computation

If a(n) is congruent to a divisor sum and the divisor sum is not divisible by the modulus, then a(n) ≠ 0. A prime p escapes this test only if 1 + pk−1 ≡ 0. Each initial segment then ends at the first prime in that bad class, and all smaller candidates are shown composite by norm_num.

The moduli do all the work, and that creates a curious asymmetry. At weight 12 the modulus 691 is small, so the sieve alone stops at 1,381, and the project needed the 62.85 GiB dense computation to get to 70,481. At weight 20 the same one-line argument reaches 1,746,109 at almost no cost. The most famous weight is the one where the cheap method is weakest.

Two details worth a second look

The modulus is not always the Bernoulli numerator

The common statement relates these congruences to the numerator of the Bernoulli number Bk. At weights 22 and 26 the honest modulus is smaller: num B₂₂ = 854513 = 11 · 77683 and num B₂₆ = 8553103 = 13 · 657931. The factors 11 and 13 divide k and cancel. They really do have to go: the table's check shows the difference at n = 2 is 27 · 77683, which is not divisible by 11, so the congruence modulo the full numerator is false. The Lean moduli are the reduced numbers num Bk / gcd(num Bk, 2k·den Bk), matching Swinnerton-Dyer's form.

Weight 26 has five bad classes

Everywhere else xk−1 ≡ −1 has one solution, x ≡ −1. At weight 26, gcd(25, 657930) = 5, so there are five solutions. They are 657930, 157708, 578462, 610260 and 627364 mod 657931, and Lean pins them down through an explicit factorization of X⁵ − 1. The first primes in the other four classes are all above 6 million, so the class −1 is still the one that ends the segment.

How one short argument gives each congruence

Pick an integral product H of E₄ and E₆ with constant term 1. Then Ek − H is a cusp form, hence a multiple of the unique eigenform. Clearing the Bernoulli denominator and cancelling a unit gives the congruence. Unlike weight 12, this needs no Sturm bound and no dense computation.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT NEW

The congruences themselves are classical (Ramanujan, Swinnerton-Dyer). What is new here, to our knowledge, is the formal proof, along with initial segments certified with no dense computation. We did not survey how far unverified computation has gone for these forms, so treat the bounds as formal bounds, not records.
