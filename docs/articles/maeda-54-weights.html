Formal verification of an open conjecture's instances

Maeda's conjecture, machine-checked in 54 weights

For every level-one weight with 2 ≤ dim S_k ≤ 10, both T₂ and T₃ have irreducible characteristic polynomials with full symmetric Galois group, proved from the Hecke operator itself.

WHAT IS PROVED

For each of the 54 level-one weights with 2 ≤ dim Sk ≤ 10, and for both T₂ and T₃: the characteristic polynomial of the actual Hecke operator on the actual space of cusp forms is irreducible over ℚ, and its Galois group is the full symmetric group Sₙ.

Maeda's conjecture (1997) says the Hecke algebra of level-one cusp forms is as large and as disordered as it could possibly be: for every weight, the characteristic polynomial of T₂ is irreducible with Galois group Sₙ. It would mean that eigenforms in a given weight are all conjugate to one another, and it is used as an input in several other areas. It remains open.

T₃ follows the same irreducibility pipeline, and its Galois groups use Frobenius certificates at every dimension from 3 up, with a separate quadratic lemma at dimension 2. Weight 132 has dimension 11 and lies just outside this range.

The smallest case, weight 24

On the two-dimensional space S₂₄, Lean proves that T₂ has matrix [[696, 1], [20736000, 384]] and characteristic polynomial

X² − 1080 X − 20468736

Its roots are 540 ± 12√144169, so the Hecke field is ℚ(√144169). The square-free parts for the six dimension-2 weights are 144169, 18209, 51349, 18295489, 2356201 and 63737521. T₃ on the same space has polynomial X² − 339480 X − 19020146544.

Why a formal version needed a new pipeline

Computing these polynomials is easy for a computer algebra system. Making a proof checker confirm them is harder, because every link has to be proved.

  1. From the operator to a table. T_p is built from scratch on Mathlib's cusp forms. Its coefficient formula (T_p f)m = fpm + pk−1[p ∣ m] fm/p reads the matrix off q-expansions of the basis ΔiE₄aE₆b.
  2. From the table to a matrix. charpoly_dimN does back-substitution for any dimension and any endomorphism. Its dimension-generic design later made T₃ a matter of one new coefficient lemma and new data.
  3. From the matrix to a polynomial. Determinants are interpolated at integer points and checked by the kernel with CharpolyEngine.
  4. From the polynomial to irreducibility. A Rabin test modulo a prime ℓ, lifted by Gauss's lemma.

The generated files never get edited by hand. Regenerating them reproduces each one byte for byte, which keeps the certificates auditable. The numbers get large: T₃ charpoly coefficients reach 317 digits at weight 134. The dimension 6 to 10 files are light modules, which meet the project's strict coding rules with no raised limits. Only the S₅ resolvent chain at dimension 5 is heavy and built separately.

WHAT THIS IS, AND WHAT IT IS NOT

It does not settle Maeda's conjecture. Ordinary computation has checked T₂ up to at least weight 12,000 (Ghitza and McAndrew), and combined with a theorem of Bengoechea that gives all T_p up to the same weight. So the 54 facts were not in doubt.

What is new is the formal chain of trust. The result starts from the analytic definition of a modular form and ends in a statement about LinearMap.charpoly, with no computer algebra system in the trusted base. We found no earlier formalization, and the project's review describes this as, to its knowledge, the largest formally verified fragment of the conjecture.

What the audit says

The combined audit at the close of Iteration 22 reports 1,312 decide certificates in the light library, none depending on more than propext. The top-level Galois theorems use the standard three axioms.
