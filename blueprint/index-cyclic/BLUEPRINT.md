## 0. Objective / non-objectives

**Objective.**

* **(i) Generic cyclicity module** `MathProof/ModularForms/HeckeCyclic.lean`
  (light; imports `HeckeLatticeIndex`, `HeckeTM`, `HeckeIndex`):
  - `quotient_span_of_le_four`: for `n ≤ 4` (hypothesis on the family),
    `heckeLattice k n ≤ t2Lattice T2 n ⊔ span ℤ {T₃}` — via the explicit
    reductions `T₁ = 1`, `T₂`, `T₄ = T₂² − 2^{k−1}·T₁` (from
    `heckeTM_mul 2 2`), each generator of 𝕋 a member; hence the quotient
    group `G := 𝕋 ⧸ ℤ[T₂]` (AddSubgroup quotient) is generated by `[T₃]`
    (surjective `ℤ → G`, `1 ↦ [T₃]` — or `ZMod D →+ G` below).
  - `order_of_t3`: the reduced denominator `D` of a T₃ witness
    (`D • T₃ = ∑ N_j • T₂^j`, Bézout-reduced as in `HeckeRational`) is the
    ORDER of `[T₃]` in `G`: `D • [T₃] = 0` from the identity; minimality
    from the Bézout reduction (generic lemma over any such witness pair —
    mirror `den_dvd_index`'s coefficientwise cross-multiplication).
  - `card_dvd_of_generator`: surjective group hom `ZMod D →+ G` from
    `D • [T₃] = 0` + generator ⟹ `Nat.card G ∣ D` (kernel + Lagrange:
    `Fintype.card` of the quotient; find names at the pin, cf. F5).
  - **`D_eq_Ik`**: combine `Nat.card G = Ik.natAbs` (b1's `index_card` +
    `relIndex` unfolding), `Ik.natAbs ∣ D` (the above), `D ∣ Ik` (LANDED:
    `HeckeIndexWeightK.D_dvd_Ik` per weight — check its exact form at G1),
    and positivity of `D` (parse check) ⟹ `D = Ik` (as ℤ, up to the sign
    conventions recorded in b1's G1 audit).
* **(ii) The iff** `heckeLattice_eq_t2Lattice_iff`:
  `𝕋 = ℤ[T₂] (as submodules) ↔ relIndex … = 1` — from `ℤ[T₂] ⊆ 𝕋` plus
  (`relIndex = 1 ↔ quotient trivial ↔ equality`); then the per-weight
  statements: at the six dim-2 weights `Ik.natAbs = 1` ⟹ **`𝕋 = ℤ[T₂]`**
  (this upgrades the iter-22 "T-Rat dim-2" numerics to an equality of
  lattices), and at the other 48 weights `Ik.natAbs ≠ 1` (small per-weight
  decide against the landed literal) ⟹ `𝕋 ≠ ℤ[T₂]`.
* **(iii) Generated instances** `HeckeCyclicWeights.lean` (dims 3–4, 12
  weights; new mode of `tools/gen_hecke_index.py` or a small sibling
  generator — keep `--check` byte-identity everywhere): per weight
  `D_eq_Ik_weightK : D = Ik` wiring the landed `D` (`HeckeRatWeightK`
  cert/scaled_id parse) and `Ik` (`HeckeIndexWeightK`).  Dim-2 weights
  carry the equality-theorem instance instead.

**Non-objectives.**  SNF/invariant factors; non-cyclicity proofs at dims
≥ 5; D = I_k beyond dims ≤ 4 (false there, F2); ring generators (b3);
Cramer witnesses for new primes.
